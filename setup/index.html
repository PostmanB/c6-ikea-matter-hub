04

Clock and Apple Home · optional

Your remotes work without Wi-Fi. Connect the ESP to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi to restore its clock after power loss and to control the light in Apple Home. Internet is used only for time sync; the light commands stay local.

Connect a hub with the experimental Wi-Fi/HomeKit firmware to configure these features.
Select a nearby network, or enter a hidden network’s name below.

Credentials go directly to the ESP over USB and are saved there. This page never uploads or saves them on your computer. Leave the password empty only for an open network.

Keep a note of your PIN. The ESP saves a verifier, so it cannot show the PIN later. Use a different PIN for each hub; avoid repeated digits and 12345678.

  1. Save Wi-Fi, choose your PIN, optionally set a schedule, then restart the ESP.
  2. Connect your iPhone to the same home network. In Home, choose Add Accessory → More Options → C6 IKEA Light.
  3. Choose Add Anyway for this DIY accessory, enter your PIN, and name the light.
  4. Test light controls in Home and a Siri command. Then check all three physical remotes.

No HomePod or Apple TV is needed for local HomeKit control. This schedule runs on the ESP. Apple Home’s own automations and away-from-home control use a home hub. After a power cut, the ESP waits for time sync; a missed turn-on runs only within two minutes of its set time.

05

Put everything in its place

Finish pairing and check all three remotes first.

  1. Disconnect this page and unplug the ESP from the computer.
  2. Power the ESP from a USB charger through its UART / USB-UART socket. That was the reliable charger-power socket on the tested board.
  3. Keep the bulb powered. Move the remotes to the door, desk and bedside.
  4. Allow up to 90 seconds for reconnection. Two bulb flashes indicate that the hub is ready. Pressing a remote during startup skips those flashes.
  5. Test all three remotes again in their final locations.

Pairings survive power loss. Keep the ESP within Thread radio range. The tested firmware supports one KAJPLATS bulb and three two-button BILRESAs; it doesn’t need this page after setup.

Connection help and diagnostics

If no firmware status appears, check the native USB socket, close other serial monitors and press the ESP’s RESET button once. Keep a sleepy remote awake during pairing.

Only selected hub messages are shown. Matter numbers are hidden. No logs or codes are uploaded.
