Pair one device at a time

Each device joins this ESP’s own network. Start with the bulb, then add the remotes. Use the printed Matter number, not IKEA’s direct-pairing procedure.

Connect the ESP to begin. Device numbers stay in this page’s memory and are sent only over USB.

04

Put everything in its place

Finish pairing and check all three remotes first.

  1. Disconnect this page and unplug the ESP from the computer.
  2. Power the ESP from a USB charger through its UART / USB-UART socket. That was the reliable charger-power socket on the tested board.
  3. Keep the bulb powered. Move the remotes to the door, desk and bedside.
  4. Allow up to 90 seconds for reconnection. Two bulb flashes indicate that the hub is ready. Pressing a remote during startup skips those flashes.
  5. Test all three remotes again in their final locations.

Pairings survive power loss. Keep the ESP within Thread radio range. The tested firmware supports one KAJPLATS bulb and three two-button BILRESAs; it doesn’t need this page after setup.

Connection help and diagnostics

If no firmware status appears, check the native USB socket, close other serial monitors and press the ESP’s RESET button once. Keep a sleepy remote awake during pairing.

Only selected hub messages are shown. Matter numbers are hidden. No logs or codes are uploaded.
